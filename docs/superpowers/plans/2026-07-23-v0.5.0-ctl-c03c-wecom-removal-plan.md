## Outcome

Physically remove the owned WeCom bot and callback product surfaces across
Core, desktop Python, Rust shell and Web: both adapters, callback crypto,
QR worker and child state, settings/onboarding producers, active messaging
documentation, imports, registries and fresh-runtime artifacts.

The leaf is non-destructive. Existing `WECOM_*` credentials, profile folders,
QR state, sessions, jobs and home targets remain readable or explicitly
removable for CTL-C07. Compatibility tokens may remain only where old state
must fail closed as `unsupported_delivery`; they must not load an adapter,
spawn a child, write new WeCom state, connect to WeCom hosts or reroute.
